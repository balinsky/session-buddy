# Domain Glossary

Definitions of the core domain terms used in Session Buddy. `Project.md` describes how these are modeled in the app; this file is the reference for what each term *means* in Irish traditional music.

**Tune.** A short piece of music that is played in an Irish music context, often in conjunction with other tunes as a set.

**Set.** A group of tunes that are played together.

**Setting.** A particular version of a tune.

**Practice.** The act of playing a tune with the goal of memorizing or improving the performance of that tune.

**Play** (as in a session — *played*, distinct from *practice*). To play through a tune, either for practice, or just in a session, where you may not have improved your playing, but probably improved your memory of it a bit more.

**Class.** A learning setting where a teacher demonstrated and taught one or more tunes.

**Teacher.** A musician who teaches tunes or instrument techniques, often both together, usually in a class, but sometimes in an informal setting.

**Musician.** A person who plays music.

**Instrument.** A device for making musical sounds.

**Note.** A sound on a particular tone that is part of a tune.

**Chord.** A group of sounds made together at one instant of time that are part of the harmony accompaniment of a tune.

**Score.** A written record or guide of notes that describe how the melody notes of a tune are played, sometimes accompanied by the harmonic chords.

**Location** (where a tune was learned or played). A location where tunes are played or classes held. Could be a virtual location, like an online class in which the participating musicians are present together in time, but not necessarily in geographical space.
